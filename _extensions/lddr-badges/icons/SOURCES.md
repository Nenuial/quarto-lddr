# Icon sources

- `cc-by-nc-sa.svg`, `cc-by-nc-sa-80x15.svg`: official Creative Commons
  buttons, https://mirrors.creativecommons.org/presskit/buttons/
  (CC trademark policy: https://creativecommons.org/policies/#trademark).
- `mmm-*.svg`: Me & My Machine labels by Fontys University of Applied
  Sciences, https://mmmlabel.tech/ ("completely free to use in any
  context"; Fontys asks users to tell them their organisation and country).
  Levels: manmade (made without GenAI), handyman (assisted by GenAI),
  cyborg (made together with GenAI), piggybacker (mostly made by GenAI),
  lazyprompter (made by GenAI).
- The `mmm-*.svg` viewBox is widened from `0 0 128 128` to `-2 -2 132 132`: the drawings reach the
  edge of the original box (up to 0.3 beyond), which clipped their outer outline in print.
